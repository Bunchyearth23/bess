# First hybrid audio pipeline — September 22, 2026 verification

## Delivered and observed

- `dist/BESS-0.2.exe`, SHA256 `D14CD7EAC1EAA50CDBFFAC042B43A9B1D3C0E751EBB37C86842A6CAFFD27103E`.
- 13 passing tests: 6 unit, 5 hybrid, 2 MVP. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` and `cargo fmt --check` passed after final formatting.
- Successfully imported 56 float32 WAVs from a real ZIP: two load layers of 28 rpm points from 803 to 4,989. Playback used the bank, not a generic generated engine.
- Two 16-second renders under `output/hybrid-ab`, each 768,000 mono samples at 48 kHz PCM24. Both RMS values were 0.030415, with <0.01 dB difference; respective peaks 0.196243 and 0.196483, without clipping. Equal RMS is not equal LUFS loudness.
- Sound BlasterX G6 computation at 192 kHz stereo for five seconds with the real bank: 500 callbacks, 3.204 ms maximum CPU and no budget overrun. Output volume was zero; this does not prove artifact-free listening. See `hybrid-audio-check.txt`.
- Window opened with vehicle imported and enhanced mode selected. An inspected egui capture showed controls and loading. Older remote-control tool actions did not prove a complete user workflow in this version. The French-interface capture is excluded publicly.
- Graft rebuilt with 110 nodes, 96 links and 13 files; deterministic graph current.

## Hybrid tests

- Periodic signal loops without a large join discontinuity.
- Two renders from a known test bank are exactly repeatable.
- A/B transition is bounded and stop fade works.
- Parameters and SHA256 source reference survive a v2 project.
- Allocation count was zero over 48,000 reads with rpm changes. This covers `Hybrid::next/set`, not transport/device dependencies.
- Response, attack, body, bite, texture, exhaust, release, turbo, micro-variation and six acoustic controls had measurable effects. Signal difference alone does not prove realistic settings.

## Conclusion and remaining work

This delivery replaced the simple main source with Automation material and offered initial editable dynamics. Subjective “better than Automation” preference remained open. The A original comparison was prepared playback of exported WAVs, not a capture of Automation's sound engine.

Still needed: per-cylinder/bank model, finer load maps, active-part identification, header-acoustic validation, behavior beyond the recorded range, events and BeamNG adaptation. The source contained exhaust only; intake and mechanical sound were reconstructed and should not be described as isolated physical tracks. Research and access limits for publications are recorded in [research](../ENGINE-SOUND-RESEARCH.md); no third-party paper code was copied.
